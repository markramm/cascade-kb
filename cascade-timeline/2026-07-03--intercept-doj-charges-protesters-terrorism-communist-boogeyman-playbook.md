The Intercept's July 3 investigation documents the Trump DOJ's systematic use of domestic-terrorism charges against protesters and dissidents, framing it as an update of Cold War tactics that labeled political opposition as subversive. An April 30 Raskin letter to DOJ had already flagged targeting of SPLC and First Amendment concerns; AG Bondi's December 2025 domestic-terrorism directive ([[ag-bondi-issues-domestic-terrorism-memo-expanding-definition-to-include-immigration-protest-and-anti-capitalism]]) supplied the legal vehicle. Combined with the FBI's Arctic Frost program targeting 400+ Republican organizations, the DOJ is running parallel targeting operations against both left-wing protesters and political opponents.

The pattern extends the domestic-terrorism-framework line already documented in the Prairieland prosecutions ([[2025-10-16--doj-unseals-first-antifa-terrorism-charges-prairieland]], [[2026-03-13--prairieland-jury-convicts-eight-material-support-terrorism]]) from a single case into a described systemic practice, landing the same week as the July 4 No Kings nationwide demonstrations ([[2026-07-04--no-kings-july-4-2026-nationwide-demonstrations-resistance-convergence]]) that this charging environment now frames.
