Trump's annual financial disclosure shows an estimated $1.4 billion in cryptocurrency earnings — over $500 million from World Liberty Financial (co-founded by Trump family members) and over $600 million from Trump-branded meme coin sales. House Judiciary Committee Democrats simultaneously released a formal report documenting foreign governments and corporate interests directing money into Trump family crypto ventures and receiving measurable regulatory benefits in return: SEC enforcement halts, favorable GENIUS Act language, and White House deregulatory signals. Former White House ethics attorney Richard Painter called the arrangement an unprecedented conflict of interest.

The disclosure is the documentary anchor for a chain of subsequent events the timeline tracked through July: the UAE's Commerce Department export-control easing that followed Sheikh Tahnoon's $500M WLF stake ([[2026-07-10--commerce-eases-uae-mgx-export-controls-after-trump-wlf-stake]]) and the CLARITY Act ethics provision collapse two weeks later, which left this same WLF revenue stream untouched ([[2026-07-23--clarity-act-ethics-provision-collapse-wlf-loophole]]) — the disclosure that revealed the scale of the conflict is the same conflict every subsequent regulatory action either advanced or failed to check.
