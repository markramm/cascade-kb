Leon County, TX commissioners voted July 14 to reject Crusoe Technologies' initial tax abatement application for an AI compute data center, citing an incomplete submission. More than 200 residents attended the meeting in opposition. Crusoe Technologies is an AI compute company — not a named hyperscaler, but among the largest AI infrastructure buildout projects in Texas by dollar value at a proposed $34 billion. Commissioners had earlier considered a reinvestment zone on prime farmland in the Marquez area. A community public hearing was set for July 27, with resubmission expected — the rejection is procedural (an incomplete submission), not a substantive denial, so the fight continues.

This is the largest single-project tax abatement fight in Texas currently active, and the farmland-conversion element places it alongside other 2026 datacenter sitings the timeline has tracked where agricultural land is the contested resource — the procedural rejection buys time for organizing rather than resolving the underlying fight.
