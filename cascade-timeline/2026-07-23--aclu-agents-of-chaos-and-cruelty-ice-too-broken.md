The ACLU released "Agents of Chaos and Cruelty," a data-driven civil rights review of immigration enforcement across eight states in 2025. The report finds ICE so systemically broken — in documentation practices, use-of-force accountability, and detainee care — that the ACLU is calling for the agency to be replaced entirely by a new agency operating under a clean charter. "They are going to kill somebody," one ACLU researcher said in characterizing the conditions documented.

The report is an institutional-level synthesis of the pattern the timeline has tracked incident-by-incident through 2026 — the record-pace in-custody death toll ([[2026-07-16--arenas-silva-death-irwin-county-ice-transfer-georgia]]) and the disputed-account fatal shootings ([[2026-07-24--intercept-four-ice-deaths-week-salgado-araujo-hearing]]) are both cited within the same eight-state dataset the ACLU used to conclude that ICE's structural accountability failures, not isolated agent misconduct, are the underlying cause.
