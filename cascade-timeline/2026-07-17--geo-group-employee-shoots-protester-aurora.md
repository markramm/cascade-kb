An employee at GEO Group's Aurora Contract Detention Facility — the same firm awarded the $529M Hudson/Big Horn contract the same week ([[2026-07-09--geo-group-529m-hudson-colorado-big-horn-detention-contract]]) — shot a woman protesting outside the facility on the night of July 16. Aurora Police confirmed the arrest. The shooting follows ICE's fatal killings of two immigrant men in Maine and Texas earlier in July, both cases in which passenger or witness testimony contradicted ICE's official account ([[2026-07-24--intercept-four-ice-deaths-week-salgado-araujo-hearing]]).

Aurora is the site of the country's first federal immigration-detention contract, awarded to GEO Group's predecessor Wackenhut in 1987 ([[1987-01-01--wackenhut-corrections-first-immigration-detention-contract]]). This is the first documented incident of contractor-on-protester violence at a GEO Group site in the current expansion cycle, occurring the same week GEO Group secured its largest 2026 contract award — pairing detention-footprint growth with escalating violence risk at the facility perimeter.
