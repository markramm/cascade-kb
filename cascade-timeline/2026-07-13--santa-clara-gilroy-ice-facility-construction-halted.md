Santa Clara County and the State of California jointly sued ICE over a detention facility under construction near Gilroy on agricultural-zoned land — built without environmental review under NEPA and without local input under the Intergovernmental Cooperation Act. On July 13, Judge Eumi Lee (N.D. Cal.) approved a consent agreement pausing all construction until September 9; ICE characterized the concession as a "compromise." An injunction hearing is set for September 8, the moment that will determine whether the pause becomes binding.

The suit follows a regional coalition-building pattern the timeline has tracked since spring 2026, including Monterey County's resolution opposing the facility ([[2026-05-19--monterey-county-resolution-opposing-gilroy-ice-detention-facility]]) and Marin County joining the state's amicus coalition ([[2026-06-23--marin-county-joins-ca-ag-gilroy-ice-facility-amicus-coalition]]) — this is the first case in that coalition-building sequence to produce an actual construction halt, however temporary, rather than a resolution or amicus filing.
