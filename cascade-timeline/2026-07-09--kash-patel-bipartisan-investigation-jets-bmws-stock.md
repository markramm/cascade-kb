Senate Judiciary Chair Chuck Grassley (R-IA) privately demanded FBI Director Kash Patel account for taxpayer-funded aircraft use and an unexplained armored BMW fleet procurement; on July 9, House Judiciary Democrats Jamie Raskin and Dick Durbin escalated by launching a formal bicameral investigation, citing whistleblower accounts of helicopter tours and jet-ski excursions conducted on FBI aircraft. A belated financial disclosure also revealed Patel failed to timely report a substantial MicroStrategy stock purchase, raising potential STOCK Act exposure. A Republican committee chair breaking with the administration to question the FBI director's spending is a notable signal of institutional strain within the governing coalition.

This extends the documented pattern of Patel's personal use of FBI resources already in the timeline — the $60M jet used for a date night ([[2025-10-25--kash-patel-fbi-plane-date-night-hypocrisy]]) and the SWAT-team girlfriend-security assignment ([[2025-11-17--kash-patel-assigns-fbi-swat-team-girlfriend-security]]) — but marks the first bipartisan congressional response to that pattern, rather than one-off reporting.
