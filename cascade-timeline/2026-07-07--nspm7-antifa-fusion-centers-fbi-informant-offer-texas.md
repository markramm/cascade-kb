Two Intercept investigations published July 7, 2026 document Trump's NSPM-7 antifa designation being operationalized at the local level. The Miami-Dade Sheriff's Office sent fusion-center bulletins framing antifa as a domestic terror threat, reproduced in the reporting. Separately, FBI and DHS agents raided a Midlothian, Texas activist's home on June 29, then offered her $200,000 to become an informant targeting "antifa networks" — both named and corroborated by the reporting. The two tracks — fusion-center intelligence bulletins feeding local police, and direct cash offers to convert targeted individuals into informants — describe NSPM-7 functioning as infrastructure for politically-targeted intelligence collection rather than a narrowly scoped counter-terrorism designation.

This extends the domestic-terrorism-framework arc the timeline already tracks through AG Bondi's December 2025 memo expanding the definition to include immigration protest and anti-capitalism ([[ag-bondi-issues-domestic-terrorism-memo-expanding-definition-to-include-immigration-protest-and-anti-capitalism]]) and the Prairieland terrorism prosecutions ([[2025-10-16--doj-unseals-first-antifa-terrorism-charges-prairieland]]), moving from prosecution of a specific case to a documented dual-track recruitment/surveillance infrastructure operating nationwide.
