The Kent County, Delaware Levy Court voted unanimously on July 21 to pass the county's first data center ordinance, restricting facilities to industrial zones with 300-foot setbacks from residential areas, closed-loop cooling requirements, and natural screening buffers. About 200 residents testified over two hours before the vote; amendments are still to come. The ordinance follows Leon County, FL's 18-month moratorium (July 14) and the broader national wave of county-level data center regulation.

Kent County's ordinance is a regulatory framework rather than a moratorium — permanent siting rules rather than a temporary pause on new applications — placing it alongside Escambia County's outright land-code ban ([[2026-07-24--escambia-county-fl-data-centers-not-permitted-vote]]) as one of the more durable regulatory responses the timeline has tracked, compared to the many fixed-term moratoriums passed elsewhere in 2026.
