The Corrections Center of Northwest Ohio (CCNO) in Stryker/Defiance County is holding ICE detainees under an active contract while community opposition intensifies. At the July 22 board meeting, the board refused public comment from the Social & Immigration Justice League of Ohio, faith leaders, and the Bowling Green Raging Grannies, citing a policy requiring five business days' advance notice to be placed on the agenda. The board also relocated its meetings from the CCNO facility in Stryker to the county emergency operations building in Defiance, specifically to reduce protester access. The ICE contract remains active.

The venue-change-to-reduce-access tactic here is a governance-level suppression mechanism distinct from the transparency erasures the timeline has tracked at the federal level ([[2026-07-21--ice-data-blackout-since-april-2026]]) — this is a local governing board using facially neutral procedural rules to limit the public's ability to contest an active ICE detention contract, worth watching as a template other facility boards may adopt.
