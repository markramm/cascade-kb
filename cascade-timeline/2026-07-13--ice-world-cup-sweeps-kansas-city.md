ICE conducted enforcement sweeps across the Kansas City metro during World Cup matches, detaining at least 30 residents. Operations included a July 9 raid on La Fontanella Foods, a food-manufacturing warehouse, in what KCUR documented as deliberate operations timed to World Cup enforcement zones. The arrests follow ICE's national surge of roughly 10,000 arrests in five days (June 25–July 1), pushing the total US detention population to approximately 39,000 — near the agency's claimed operational capacity before its next round of facility activations.

Kansas City passed a five-year moratorium on non-municipal detention facility use in January 2026 ([[2026-01-15--kansas-city-council-moratorium-ice-detention-platform-ventures]]) — this enforcement sweep shows that a local moratorium on new detention infrastructure does not constrain ICE's field-arrest operations in the same jurisdiction, a distinction worth tracking as more cities pass similar detention-facility moratoriums expecting broader protective effect.
