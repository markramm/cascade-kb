ICE is approaching 600,000 cumulative deportations since January 2025, with July 2026 arrests averaging 1,593 per day — surpassing June's record of 43,000 monthly arrests, itself the highest single-month total in the agency's 23-year history. As of July 11, ICE held 65,765 people in detention. Two immigrants were killed in ICE traffic-enforcement incidents in Texas and Maine in July ([[2026-07-07--ice-kills-lorenzo-salgado-araujo-houston-narrative-collapse]], [[2026-07-13--ice-fatal-shooting-biddeford-maine-weaponize-vehicle]]). Federal courts continue releasing roughly 2,000 detainees per month through court-ordered bond hearings.

The record-pace arrest and detention figures land the same week ICE's own detention and deportation reporting mandate was confirmed to have gone dark since April 2026 ([[2026-07-21--ice-data-blackout-detention-deportation-stats]]) — the agency's enforcement scale is now documented mainly through court filings, congressional disclosures, and independent tracking rather than the agency's own statistics.
