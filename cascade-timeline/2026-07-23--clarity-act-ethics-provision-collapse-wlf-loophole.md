Senate Republicans released a CLARITY Act revision on July 22 containing the bill's first ethics language — but enforcement rests solely with Trump's own DOJ, World Liberty Financial's token-fee revenue is left untouched, and the provision expires when Trump leaves office. Seven Democrats (Cortez Masto, Alsobrooks, Booker, Gallego, Hickenlooper, Warner, Warnock) rejected the language within hours. Senate Majority Leader Thune said the pending August recess forecloses passage this session; the White House told Democrats to "accept the victory" while the underlying $2.3 billion family conflict ([[2026-06-09--trump-family-2-3b-crypto-income-wlfi-national-bank-charter]]) remains structurally intact.

This is the first legislative attempt at crypto ethics language written around, rather than against, the specific architecture of Trump's family crypto holdings — self-enforcing, self-expiring, and exempting the largest revenue stream. It lands the same week Trump's financial disclosure confirmed $1.4 billion in 2025 crypto gains ([[2026-07-08--trump-14b-crypto-gains-foreign-pay-to-play]]), tying the ethics-provision collapse directly to the disclosure that motivated it.
