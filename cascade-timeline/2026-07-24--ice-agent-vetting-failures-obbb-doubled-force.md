Documented (NY) reports that law enforcement quality experts are raising alarms about ICE's accelerated hiring vetting process as the agency doubled its agent corps from roughly 10,000 to 22,000 under One Big Beautiful Bill mandates ([[2026-07-04--dhs-obbb-year-one-165b-doubled-ice-force-accountability-gap]]). The specific concerns are compressed background-check timelines, lowered fitness standards, and the absence of the psychological screening requirements standard in municipal police hiring.

This applies the same OBBB-era accountability-gap pattern the timeline has tracked in contracting and detention capacity to personnel quality specifically — an agency doubling in size on a compressed timeline is documented to be doing so with weakened, not equivalent, hiring standards, a structural precondition for the record pace of fatal field incidents and in-custody deaths the timeline has tracked through July 2026.
