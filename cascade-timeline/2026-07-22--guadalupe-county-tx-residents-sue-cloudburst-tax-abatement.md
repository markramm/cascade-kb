A resident group filed suit July 22 challenging Guadalupe County, TX commissioners' $500M tax abatement for CloudBurst Texas's 12-building AI data center campus in New Braunfels. The commissioners court had rejected the abatement 3-2 in February, then reversed and approved it in April; the lawsuit argues the reversal and the resulting tax breaks were procured unlawfully.

The specific legal theory is the reversal itself — a rejected deal that comes back for a second vote and passes months later, absent a disclosed change in circumstances, is the same fact pattern the timeline should watch for at other contested abatement sites, since it raises an inference of undisclosed lobbying or inducement between the two votes rather than a routine change of commissioner mind.
