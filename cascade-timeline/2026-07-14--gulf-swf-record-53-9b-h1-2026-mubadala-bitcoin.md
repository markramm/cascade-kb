Gulf sovereign wealth funds committed a record $53.9 billion across 108 deals in H1 2026, with nearly half directed into the United States and technology dominating deal activity. Abu Dhabi's Mubadala invested $12.9 billion into AI and digitalization and held 14.7 million shares of spot Bitcoin ETFs worth approximately $566 million as of March 31. The record deployment consolidates a pattern of Gulf states — UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar — using sovereign capital to build structural influence over US AI infrastructure and crypto markets, landing in the same period as Trump's $142B Saudi arms deal and the Trump family's documented UAE-linked crypto investments.

This is the macro figure behind the individual deals the timeline has tracked piecemeal in 2026, including Commerce's export-control easing that followed Sheikh Tahnoon's $500M World Liberty Financial stake ([[2026-07-10--commerce-eases-uae-mgx-export-controls-after-trump-wlf-stake]]) — the aggregate $53.9B shows that instance is one node in a much larger capital-deployment pattern, not an isolated transaction.
